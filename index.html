Rules from the scorecard
Bidding
  • Bid 0 and take no tricks: +0
  • Bid 0 and take any trick: −1
  • Make your bid exactly: + your bid
  • Miss your bid, over or under: − your bid
  • Total bids can never equal the cards dealt, so someone always misses. The dealer bids last and is the one held to it.
Trump edition
  • After dealing, flip the top card of the remaining deck. Its suit is trump for that round.
No Look
  • One card each. Bid without looking at your card.
Ties
  • If the top score is tied at the end, play an OT round.
